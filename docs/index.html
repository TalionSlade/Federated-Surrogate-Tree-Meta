FedRF-Distill v0.1.0 • research-grade

Privacy-preserving cross-framework federated distillation

A protocol-driven framework for tree-based federated learning with formal (ε, δ)-differential privacy on soft labels (Hook A) and cross-framework heterogeneity across sklearn / XGBoost / LightGBM / CatBoost (Hook B). Designed for submission to A* venues.

10 global benchmark datasets
4 DP mechanisms
4 partitioners (IID → pathological)
3 aggregation strategies
Empirical MIA validation
