Mathematical Foundation
One round = 7 typed transformations
▌ WHAT — type signature of a round
Round \(t\) is a sequence of total functions across 3 swim-lanes:
$$\begin{aligned} \text{fit:} \quad & (X_k, y_k) \mapsto \mathcal{T}_k \\ \text{soft:} \quad & \mathcal{T}_k(X_k) \mapsto P_k \in \Delta^{K-1} \\ \text{DP:} \quad & \mathcal{M}(P_k;\, \varepsilon, \delta) \mapsto \tilde{P}_k \\ \text{distil:} \quad & (X_k, \tilde{P}_k) \mapsto S_k \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} \text{agg:} \quad & \{S_k\}_{k=1}^{K} \mapsto G \\ \text{ledger:} \quad & (\varepsilon, \delta) \mapsto (\varepsilon_{\text{cum}}, \delta_{\text{cum}}) \\ \text{stack:} \quad & (X_k, y_k, G) \mapsto M_k \end{aligned}$$
# Invariant: \(\tilde{P}_k \in \Delta^{K-1}\) after clip + renorm
▌ WHY — this exact shape
Privacy must be injected once and only once. Putting DP between the teacher's raw soft labels and the student's training data means every downstream object — the surrogate, the aggregate, the meta-refined model — inherits (ε, δ)-DP for free by post-processing.

Step 5 averages on a proxy because client surrogates have incomparable internals (different splits, different feature thresholds) — but they all map X → Δk-1. The probability simplex is the canonical common ground.

Step 7 happens after broadcast so the local model can use the global as an evidence feature, not blindly replace itself.
▌ MINDSET — why a fixed cadence
A federated round is the atomic unit of trust: clients commit a privatised distillation, coordinator commits an aggregated distillation, accountant commits a ledger entry. Either all 7 steps succeed or the round is rolled back.

No partial trust. Coordinator never sees a raw soft label; client never trusts the global until the artifact's class path is verified by safe_deserialize.

Bound the surface, not the depth. 7 steps × T rounds is small; the depth of each tree is small. The framework's power comes from composing simple privatised steps, not from a single heroic algorithm.

VARIABLE REFERENCE · ROUND-LEVEL ALGEBRA

Every symbol used in the round map above — what it is, where it lives, how it's computed

Symbol
What it represents
Where it lives / how it's computed
Local data & teacher (client side)
\(X_k, y_k\)
Private feature matrix and label vector on client \(k\); shapes \((n_k, d)\) and \((n_k,)\). Never crosses the wire.
FederatedClient.X_local, FederatedClient.y_local — set in the constructor from a Partitioner shard.
\(n_k, d\)
Row count on client \(k\); feature dimensionality (constant across all clients).
X_k.shape[0] and X_k.shape[1]. \(n_k\) is reported in ClientUpdate.n_samples.
\(\mathcal{T}_k\)
Local teacher fit on \((X_k, y_k)\) — typically a Random Forest / XGBoost / LightGBM.
self.teacher = self.teacher_factory(); self.teacher.fit(X_local, y_local) in client.local_train().
Soft labels & DP privatisation
\(P_k\)
Raw teacher posteriors on the client's own data; shape \((n_k, |\mathcal{C}|)\), row-stochastic.
P = self.teacher.predict_proba(self.X_local) via SoftLabelGenerator.generate.
\(\Delta^{K-1}\)
The \((|\mathcal{C}|-1)\)-dimensional probability simplex — non-negative entries summing to 1. Each row of \(P_k\) or \(\tilde{P}_k\) lives here.
Maintained by clip(.,0,1) then row-normalisation in privacy/mechanisms.py.
\(\mathcal{M}\)
DP mechanism (LaplaceMechanism, GaussianMechanism, NullDPMechanism) — the only randomised step.
Injected via cfg.dp.mechanism; built in _build_dp_mechanism(runner).
\(\varepsilon, \delta\)
Per-round privacy budget pair. \(\varepsilon\) bounds divergence between neighbouring datasets; \(\delta\) the failure probability.
cfg.dp.target_epsilon / n_rounds and cfg.dp.target_delta.
\(\tilde{P}_k\)
Privatised soft labels — sole noised object in the pipeline. Same shape as \(P_k\), still row-stochastic.
Returned by self.dp_mechanism.privatize(P, ε, δ). Computed as \(\mathrm{clip}_{[0,1]}(P+\eta) / \mathrm{rowsum}\).
Distillation & aggregation
\(S_k\)
Per-client surrogate tree (≈10 KB) fit on \((X_k, \tilde{P}_k)\). The wire payload.
S_k = self.distiller.distill(X_local, P̃, classes) — returns a BaseModelAdapter.
\(\{S_k\}_{k=1}^{K}\)
Collection of all client surrogates received this round.
Coordinator receives them as updates: list[ClientUpdate] with surrogate_blob fields.
\(G\)
Global surrogate distilled from the averaged client predictions on proxy_X. The broadcast model.
G = aggregator.aggregate(updates, proxy_X, classes) → wrapped in GlobalArtifact.
Privacy ledger & meta-refinement
\(\varepsilon_{\text{cum}}, \delta_{\text{cum}}\)
Cumulative privacy spend across all rounds so far. Used to enforce the target budget.
coordinator.accountant.cumulative(). Basic composition: linear; RDP: \(\sqrt{R}\)-scaling.
\(M_k\)
Client \(k\)'s refined local model after stacking; wrapped as StackedAdapter(inner, G).
self.refined_model = self.meta_learner.refine(X_local, y_local, G, teacher_factory).
Invariant. The single noise injection at step DP — and the fact that every later step (distil, aggregate, stack) is a deterministic function of \(\tilde{P}_k\) and public data (proxy_X) — is what gives \(G\) and \(M_k\) their \((\varepsilon, \delta)\)-DP guarantee for free, by the post-processing closure of differential privacy.
