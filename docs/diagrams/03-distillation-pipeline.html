Formal building blocks

Temperature scaling
pᵢ(T) = pᵢ1/T / Σⱼ pⱼ1/T
T > 1 smooths the distribution to preserve "dark knowledge"; T = 1 is the identity.
Laplace mechanism
η ∼ Lap(0, Δ₁/ε), Δ₁ ≤ 2
Pure ε-DP. Δ₁ bound because one row of P swaps at most 2 mass in L1.
Gaussian mechanism
σ ≥ Δ₂·√(2·ln(1.25/δ))/ε
(ε, δ)-DP. L2 sensitivity Δ₂ ≤ √2. Composes tightly under Rényi-DP.
