RDPAccountant (Gaussian-specialised)

Rényi-DP · Mironov 2017
# For each round with noise multiplier σᵢ / Δ₂ α-RDP: ε(α) = Σᵢ α / (2 · μᵢ²) where μᵢ = σᵢ / Δ₂ # Convert RDP → (ε, δ)-DP ε_DP = minα > 1 [ ε(α) + ln(1/δ) / (α-1) ]
PROS
+ Sub-linear growth for Gaussian ⇒ many more rounds at same ε.
+ State of the art for analytic Gaussian composition.
+ α grid is automatic (1.5 … 64).
CONS
− Gaussian only (in this codebase).
− Requires recording σ per round (handled internally).

Mathematical Foundation
Composition: T(ε) vs √T(ε)
▌ WHAT — the three composition results
Composing $T$ identical $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP mechanisms yields different bounds depending on the analysis:
$\varepsilon_T = T\varepsilon,\quad \delta_T = T\delta \qquad \text{(Basic, Dwork 2006)}$
$\varepsilon_T = \sqrt{2T\ln(1/\delta')}\,\varepsilon + T\varepsilon(e^{\varepsilon}-1),\quad \delta_T = T\delta + \delta' \qquad \text{(Advanced)}$
$\varepsilon_T(\alpha) = \dfrac{T\,\alpha}{2\mu^2},\quad \varepsilon_{\mathrm{DP}} = \min_{\alpha}\!\left[\varepsilon_T(\alpha) + \dfrac{\ln(1/\delta)}{\alpha-1}\right] \qquad \text{(RDP)}$
# Asymptotic: $\varepsilon_T \sim \sqrt{T}\,\varepsilon$ ⇒ ~2.5× more rounds
▌ WHY — keep a ledger
Privacy is non-renewable. Every privatize call subtracts from the budget. The ledger is the only persistent state that survives across rounds — it has to be append-only and auditable.

The accountant decides when to stop. The coordinator's round() consults cumulative() before each release; if the cumulative budget would exceed the target, it raises PrivacyBudgetExhausted and the experiment halts cleanly.

This is the only error in the framework that cannot be caught and continued.
▌ MINDSET — pick the accountant for the noise
Basic composition is mechanism-agnostic — it works for anything. Use it when you don't know the mechanism (rare) or you want a clean upper bound for the paper.

RDP is Gaussian-specialised — it exploits the subgaussian tail to give a tight bound that scales as √T. Use it whenever the mechanism is Gaussian and you want many rounds.

Never claim a bound you can't compute. If a future mechanism doesn't fit either accountant, ship it with a new accountant — don't fudge an old one.

Basic composition (Dwork 2006)
\(\varepsilon_T = T\varepsilon\)
Linear sum of per-call \(\varepsilon\) — mechanism-agnostic, always sound, often loose.
BasicCompositionAccountant.cumulative(): sum(record.epsilon for record in self.ledger).
\(\delta_T = T\delta\)
Linear sum of per-call \(\delta\).
Same accountant — sum(record.delta for record in self.ledger).
Advanced composition
\(\sqrt{2T\ln(1/\delta')}\,\varepsilon\)
Subgaussian-style \(\sqrt{T}\)-scaling first-order term — the lead saving over basic.
Closed form in AdvancedCompositionAccountant._compute().
\(T\varepsilon(e^{\varepsilon}-1)\)
Second-order correction — vanishes as \(\varepsilon\to 0\), dominates for large per-call \(\varepsilon\).
Second additive term in the same closed form.
Rényi DP (Mironov 2017)
\(\alpha\)
Rényi order — a real \(>1\) at which Rényi divergence is computed; \(\alpha\to\infty\) recovers max-divergence (pure DP).
Swept on a grid in RDPAccountant._best_alpha() (typically [2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128]).
\(\mu\)
Gaussian noise multiplier \(\mu = \sigma/\Delta_2\) — the dimensionless ratio of std-dev to sensitivity.
Computed once per mechanism instantiation from self.sensitivity and the calibrated \(\sigma\).
\(\varepsilon_T(\alpha) = T\alpha/(2\mu^2)\)
RDP loss after \(T\) Gaussian-mechanism compositions at order \(\alpha\).
RDPAccountant._rdp_at(alpha); the \(T\alpha/(2\mu^2)\) form is exact for Gaussian.
\(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{DP}}\)
Best \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-DP bound obtained by minimising over \(\alpha\) — the headline RDP report.
RDPAccountant.cumulative() — min over alpha of (eps_T(alpha) + log(1/delta)/(alpha-1)).
\(\ln(1/\delta)/(\alpha-1)\)
RDP-to-DP conversion term — penalises low orders, vanishes as \(\alpha\to\infty\).
Added to \(\varepsilon_T(\alpha)\) before the min in cumulative().
Budget enforcement
\(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{target}}\)
User-declared total privacy budget — the contract that cannot be exceeded.
cfg.dp.target_epsilon; checked in FederatedCoordinator.round() before every release.
\(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{cum}} > \varepsilon_{\mathrm{target}}\)
Exhaustion condition — when crossed, the experiment halts.
Raises PrivacyBudgetExhausted from privacy/exceptions.py; the only unrecoverable error in the framework.
Invariant. Basic composition is mechanism-agnostic and always sound but linear in \(T\); RDP is Gaussian-specialised and sub-linear (\(\sqrt{T}\)) — at the same per-call noise, RDP buys ≈2.5× more rounds inside the same target budget.
